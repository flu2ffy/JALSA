Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers to help you get started with JALSA.

What is JALSA?

JALSA is an Arabic and English party-game website for friends and families. It brings trivia, guessing and social games together in one place.

Do I need an account to play?

Yes. Sign in to a verified JALSA account or create an account before starting a game.

Which games are available?

JALSA features Zanko, Price Guessing, Imposter, Mafia and INSIGHT. Game availability may change while a game is being updated.

Can I use JALSA in Arabic and English?

Yes. JALSA supports Arabic and English. Use the language control on the website to switch languages.

How do I start a game?

Sign in, choose a game from the JALSA homepage and follow the on-screen setup instructions.

How can I contact JALSA?

Email the JALSA team at support@jalsa.me.
