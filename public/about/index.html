Every game deserves a JALSA.

JALSA brings friends and families together with playful challenges, clever questions and shared game-night moments — in Arabic and English.

What is JALSA?

JALSA is a bilingual party-game website made for groups who want to gather, play and make memories. Choose a game, follow its on-screen setup, and enjoy a little friendly competition around the same table.
